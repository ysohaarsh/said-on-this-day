# Scope decisions (from the user, 2026-10-31)

- Keep only dates SPOKEN or NARRATED by a character/narrator. Skip on-screen-only text (title cards, captions, newspapers).
- Holidays count only when the calendar date itself is said ("December 24th", "the Fourth of July"). A bare "Christmas Eve" / "Halloween" does not count.
- Real-history dates count when spoken or narrated in the film/show (e.g. "December 7th, 1941").
- Films: Hollywood first, but Bollywood and international films are fine if they are famous.
- Shows: the user's own shows get priority over list shows (data/titles_tv_user.csv).
- No anime character birthdays. Anime only to fill empty days.
